# SEO Architecture Notes

## Implemented

- Dynamic metadata with canonical URLs
- Open Graph and Twitter metadata
- `sitemap.xml` generated from static, national, and city landing pages
- `robots.txt`
- Organization schema
- LocalBusiness schema
- Service schema
- FAQ schema
- Breadcrumb schema
- SEO-first city landing page template
- Blog hub architecture for topical authority

## City Strategy

City routes are generated using:

- `cityServices`
- `tierOneCities`
- `tierTwoCities`
- `cityPageSlugs`

Each city page includes localized headings, FAQ schema, LocalBusiness schema, service schema, breadcrumb schema, and internal conversion CTAs.
